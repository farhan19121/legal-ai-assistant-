Put PDFs (and .txt files) of Indian statutes and landmark judgments in this folder, then run:  python ingest.py

Suggested starting set (download from indiacode.nic.in / Indian Kanoon, verify each source yourself):
- Consumer Protection Act 2019
- Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020
- Consumer Protection (Jurisdiction) Rules 2021
- Indian Contract Act 1872, Companies Act 2013 (key sections), DPDP Act 2023
- 20-50 landmark consumer judgments
